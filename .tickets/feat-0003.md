## Notes

**Env names are load-bearing.** Verified against the live chart in
`homelab-gitops` (`charts/soundings/values.yaml`, `values-dev.yaml`,
`templates/api-deployment.yaml`): `S3_ENDPOINT=http://192.168.1.12:3900`,
`S3_REGION=us-east-1`, `S3_BUCKET=soundings-prod` (dev: `soundings-dev`),
`S3_FORCE_PATH_STYLE="true"` from `config:`; `S3_ACCESS_KEY_ID` and
`S3_SECRET_ACCESS_KEY` from the `soundings-app` Secret. A name mismatch would
make a fully-configured cluster report `storage: unconfigured` and fail the
`deployed` criterion silently.

**`/healthz` backs all three probes.** `api-deployment.yaml` wires
`startupProbe`, `readinessProbe` and `livenessProbe` all to `/healthz`. Adding
a Garage call there is an outage risk: with boto3's ~60s default timeouts and
retries, a Garage outage would hang liveness and crashloop every api pod in
both slots — over a dependency not yet on the critical path. Hence the
separate 2s/2s/1-retry health client, the deliberately broad `except`, and the
explicit HTTP-200 assertions in the tests as the regression guard. Do not
"clean up" the broad catch.
